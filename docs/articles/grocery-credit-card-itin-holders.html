Best Grocery Credit Cards for ITIN Holders (2026)

Quick Answer

ITIN holders can earn 3%-6% cash back on groceries with cards from American Express, Capital One, and Bank of America, all of which accept an ITIN instead of an SSN. The right card depends on your credit history: secured options exist for newcomers, while unsecured grocery cards require a score around 670 or higher.

Groceries are most people’s biggest recurring expense, and ITIN holders are no different. The good news: several of the best grocery rewards cards in the U.S. accept an ITIN instead of a Social Security number, so you can earn real cash back on every supermarket trip while also building your U.S. credit history. This guide breaks down exactly which cards work, what they earn, and how to pick the right one for where you are in your credit journey.

Can I actually earn grocery rewards with an ITIN, or am I stuck with flat-rate cards?

With an ITIN you can still apply for various types of credit cards, including cash-back cards that reward specific spending categories like groceries. The ITIN is simply your tax identification number on the application; it does not restrict you to a certain card type.

That said, the grocery cards with the highest earn rates (like 6% back) require good to excellent credit, so your realistic options depend on how long you have been building U.S. credit history. If you are brand new, start with a secured card that earns in everyday categories, graduate to an unsecured card after 6-12 months, and then reach for the premium grocery cards. What stops most first applications is an empty credit file rather than the ITIN number itself, which is why getting that first approval is the problem worth solving.

Which grocery card is best if I have no U.S. credit history yet?

This one comes up a lot: newcomers who just received their ITIN and want to earn something on groceries from day one.

If your U.S. credit file is empty or less than six months old, your two strongest options are the Capital One Quicksilver Secured and the Bank of America Customized Cash Secured.

The Capital One Quicksilver Secured pays 1.5% cash back on every purchase. Cardholders put down a $200 minimum deposit to open the account. That flat 1.5% applies to grocery runs just as it does to everything else, with no category activation required.

For a category-specific earn, Bank of America is worth a look. It accepts ITINs at the branch level and offers one of the better secured cards for rewards: 3% cash back in a category you choose, 2% at grocery stores and wholesale clubs (up to $2,500 per quarter combined), and 1% elsewhere, with no annual fee. The catch is that Bank of America only takes ITIN applicants in-branch, not online, so you have to visit a financial center in person.

Both cards report to all three bureaus, so every on-time payment moves your score in the right direction. For more details on the secured card path, visit our guide to secured credit cards with an ITIN.

Which no-annual-fee grocery card should I target after I build some credit?

Once you have 6-12 months of on-time payment history and a score approaching 670, two no-fee options become realistic.

The Capital One SavorOne earns unlimited 3% cash back at grocery stores, on dining, entertainment, and popular streaming services. The student version carries the same unlimited 3% at grocery stores with no annual fee. One thing to know: the 3% grocery earn applies to standard grocery stores and excludes superstores like Walmart and Target. Capital One confirms that an ITIN can be used in place of an SSN on the application, and you can apply fully online.

The American Express Blue Cash Everyday is the other strong contender at this tier. It earns 3% cash back at U.S. supermarkets, U.S. online retail, and U.S. gas stations, all with no annual fee. American Express’s live application help text confirms: “If you do not have an SSN, you can enter your ITIN here instead.” If your grocery and gas spending together run at least $400 a month, this card puts a meaningful amount back in your pocket each year.

Is the 6% Amex Blue Cash Preferred worth the annual fee for ITIN holders?

Readers frequently ask: whether the premium grocery card actually pays off, especially when you are still managing new-immigrant finances.

The Blue Cash Preferred earns up to 6% cash back at U.S. supermarkets (up to $6,000 per year) and 6% on select U.S. streaming subscriptions, plus 3% at U.S. gas stations and transit. It is the top grocery earner in this group. The card carries a $95 annual fee (waived the first year), so the math only works in your favor if your household spends enough at supermarkets to justify it.

Here is a simple break-even check: at 6% back versus the 3% you would earn on a no-fee alternative, you gain an extra 3 cents per dollar. To earn $95 more per year from that extra 3%, you need to spend roughly $3,167 at U.S. supermarkets annually (about $264 a month). Most households that use a grocery card as their primary everyday card clear that threshold without much trouble. Keep in mind that superstore purchases at Walmart or Target do not earn the 6% rate on this card, so if most of your grocery shopping happens there, the no-fee flat-rate option may actually serve you better.

American Express, Chase, Capital One, and Citi all allow you to open a credit card without an SSN if you have an ITIN. You will need a score of 670 or higher for the Blue Cash Preferred; if you are not there yet, the Blue Cash Everyday is the natural stepping stone.

Does Capital One Savor really earn 3% at grocery stores with an ITIN, no SSN?

Yes, and it is one of the most straightforward picks in this category. The Capital One Savor Student card is an unsecured student rewards card that offers unlimited 3% cash back at grocery stores and on dining, entertainment, and popular streaming services, plus 1% on everything else. The non-student SavorOne version carries the same earn structure with no annual fee for applicants with established credit.

The 3% grocery earn covers standard grocery stores and restaurants but excludes superstores like Walmart and Target. If your regular grocery store is a standard supermarket chain rather than a big-box retailer, this card earns consistently. For students who have an ITIN, the Savor Student version is especially accessible: it earns on groceries, dining, and entertainment with no limits and no security deposit, and foreign college students with an ITIN can apply.

How do I actually apply for a grocery card using my ITIN?

The process depends on the issuer. For Capital One and American Express, the application is fully online: enter your ITIN in the tax ID field where the form asks for an SSN. For Bank of America’s secured card, you need to apply in-branch and bring your ITIN assignment letter (IRS CP-565), a government-issued photo ID, and proof of a U.S. address.

A few practical things worth doing before you apply. First, check whether the card offers pre-qualification without a hard inquiry. See our guide on credit card pre-qualification with an ITIN for a step-by-step walkthrough. Second, make sure your ITIN is current. Unlike an SSN, which is valid for life, an ITIN requires you to file your federal income tax at least once every three years to stay valid. An expired ITIN will get an application declined for identity reasons rather than creditworthiness, which is a frustrating outcome that is easy to avoid. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a grocery rewards credit card with an ITIN and no U.S. credit history? Yes, but your options are narrower. The Capital One Quicksilver Secured and the Discover it Secured both accept an ITIN and earn cash back at grocery stores and restaurants. Build 6-12 months of history with one of these before applying for a higher-tier grocery card.

Does the American Express Blue Cash Preferred card accept an ITIN? Yes. American Express explicitly states on its application that you can enter an ITIN where an SSN is requested. The Blue Cash Preferred earns 6% at U.S. supermarkets (up to $6,000 per year), but it requires good to excellent credit, typically a score of 670 or higher.

Do Walmart and Target purchases count as groceries on these cards? Usually not. Cards like the Capital One Savor, Amex Blue Cash Preferred, and Bank of America Customized Cash all exclude superstores like Walmart and Target from their grocery bonus categories. Purchases there typically earn only the base rate of 1%.

Which ITIN-friendly grocery card has no annual fee? The Capital One Savor Cash Rewards Credit Card earns 3% at grocery stores with no annual fee and accepts an ITIN. The Amex Blue Cash Everyday also earns 3% at U.S. supermarkets with no annual fee and accepts an ITIN online.

How do I apply for a grocery rewards card with an ITIN? Enter your 9-digit ITIN in the SSN field on the online application. For Bank of America, you must apply in-branch with your ITIN document. Capital One and American Express both allow fully online ITIN applications. Bring your ITIN assignment letter (IRS CP-565) in case extra verification is requested.
