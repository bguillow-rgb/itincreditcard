Virtual Credit Cards for ITIN Holders (2026 Guide)

By Editorial Staff · Secured & Starter Cards · Published September 23, 2026

Quick Answer

Yes, ITIN holders can get virtual credit cards. Several issuers, including Zolve and certain fintech platforms, let you generate a virtual card number linked to an ITIN-accepted account. You do not need an SSN. A physical card and a virtual number typically come from the same underlying account.

If you shop online often, pay subscriptions, or just want a safer way to handle digital purchases, a virtual credit card number is worth knowing about. For ITIN holders, the short version is this: virtual card access is not locked behind an SSN. Several issuers already offer it, and the application process is nearly identical to getting any ITIN credit card. Here is what you need to know before you apply.

What exactly is a virtual credit card, and how does it work for ITIN holders?

A virtual credit card is a temporary or permanent card number generated by your issuer and linked to your existing credit account. Purchases made with a virtual number count toward your credit limit the same way physical card purchases do, as do other transactions like balance transfers. The virtual number works anywhere the underlying card network (Visa, Mastercard, etc.) is accepted online, but it keeps your real card number hidden from merchants, which cuts down on fraud risk.

For ITIN holders, the mechanics are the same. Credit bureaus track account activity by ITIN, so your file builds the same way it would with an SSN. Every on-time payment you make with a virtual number gets reported under your ITIN, contributing to your U.S. credit history just as a swipe of the physical card would.

Virtual credit cards have to be linked to a traditional credit card account. You generally will not need an SSN to get the virtual card add-on itself, but most accounts that issue virtual numbers do require an SSN or ITIN to open in the first place. So the first step is always getting approved for the underlying account.

Can I get a virtual card before I even arrive in the U.S.?

This one comes up a lot, especially from people planning a move who want to sort out their finances before they land.

Zolve is an unsecured U.S. credit card with no annual fee. It does not require an SSN, and you can apply with a passport or ITIN. The standout detail: you can apply and receive a virtual card number while still abroad, which makes it the only real option for someone who needs a card before they set foot in the country. You can use the virtual number to pay for subscriptions, book accommodations, or shop online from the day you are approved.

Zolve advertises limits up to $10,000 based on alternative underwriting, which is considerably higher than what most starter secured cards offer. Because it is unsecured, there is no deposit to fund before the virtual number becomes usable.

For everyone else applying after arriving in the U.S., most issuers generate your virtual number inside the app or online dashboard within one to three business days of approval, well before the physical card shows up in the mail.

How does Capital One’s virtual card tool work for ITIN holders?

Capital One offers a browser extension called Eno that generates virtual card numbers for online purchases. The Capital One Platinum Secured and the Capital One Quicksilver Secured both accept ITINs and are reasonable starting points for someone building credit from zero. Once approved, you install the Eno extension and it creates a unique virtual number for each merchant you shop with. If that merchant’s site is ever compromised, your actual card number stays safe.

Capital One reviews secured accounts for a credit-line increase in as little as six months of on-time payments, so your virtual card’s spending power can grow alongside your credit history. When your limit increases, the virtual card numbers reflect the updated limit automatically.

One thing worth planning for: applying in person at a Capital One branch significantly improves approval odds for ITIN holders, because the online system sometimes cannot process ITIN numbers correctly. Once you are approved in-branch, the Eno virtual card tool works the same as it does for any other cardholder.

Does using a virtual card number help or hurt my credit building?

Neither. A common misconception about secured credit cards is that they work like a debit card. They do not. Money is not subtracted from your checking account when you use a secured credit card. You receive a monthly statement and pay the bill at that point. The same logic applies whether you spend with a virtual number or a physical card: the purchase posts to your account, the statement balance is what you owe, and your payment behavior is what the bureaus see.

What actually drives credit building is consistent on-time payments and keeping your balance well below your limit. With on-time payments and utilization under 30%, most ITIN cardholders reach a scoreable credit file within 3-6 months and a 700+ score within 12-18 months. A virtual card makes it no faster or slower. It is simply a safer way to transmit your card number.

One practical tip: if you use a virtual card for recurring subscriptions, set a calendar reminder to check that the subscription did not fail after a card refresh. Some issuers rotate virtual numbers periodically for security, which can cause a subscription to decline if the merchant’s stored number goes stale.

What documents do I need to apply for an ITIN credit card that offers virtual numbers?

Readers frequently ask whether the document requirements differ for cards with virtual number features. They do not. The virtual card is an add-on to the underlying account, so the application requirements are the same as for any ITIN credit card.

If you are a recent immigrant, an international student, or another noncitizen without an SSN, you may still be able to apply for a credit card with an ITIN. Many issuers have rewards cards you can apply for using an ITIN, including Chase, American Express, and Capital One. You will still need to meet the issuer’s other requirements, though: the lender may review your credit history, request alternative forms of identification, require a checking or savings account, and factor your income into its decision.

In practice, most ITIN applicants bring their ITIN letter from the IRS, a foreign passport, proof of a U.S. address (utility bill, lease, or bank statement), and recent bank statements to show income or savings. Can I add my ITIN card to Apple Pay or Google Pay instead of getting a virtual number?

Yes, and this is often the simpler path. Adding your physical card to Apple Pay or Google Pay creates a tokenized version of your card number for contactless and in-app payments. A ghost credit card is a virtual account number for businesses that lets them monitor spending and make secure payments. For individual consumers, the equivalent is your digital wallet token. The token is unique to your device, so even if a merchant is hacked, your actual card number is not exposed.

Most ITIN-accepting cards that issue a physical card support Apple Pay and Google Pay. You add the card through your phone’s wallet app and verify with the issuer (usually a text code or a call). From that point, you can tap to pay in stores or use the wallet for online checkout without sharing your physical card number. This works for Zolve, Firstcard, Capital One secured cards, and Chase Freedom Rise, among others. FAQs

Can I get a virtual credit card with an ITIN and no U.S. credit history? Yes. Cards like the Zolve Azpire and the Current Build Card accept an ITIN (or even just a passport in Zolve’s case) and issue virtual card numbers with no U.S. credit history required. Both report to all three major bureaus.

Is a virtual card number the same as a real credit card for ITIN holders? Functionally, yes. A virtual card number is tied to your real credit account. Purchases post to your statement and are reported to credit bureaus exactly as physical card purchases are. The only difference is there is no plastic involved.

Can I add my ITIN credit card to Apple Pay or Google Pay? Most ITIN-accepting cards that issue a physical card also support Apple Pay and Google Pay. Zolve, Capital One secured cards, and the Firstcard all support digital wallet tokenization once the card is added to your device.

Does using a virtual card number instead of a physical card affect my credit building? No. Credit bureaus report on account-level activity, not on how you present the card at checkout. Paying on time and keeping utilization low matters far more than whether you used a virtual or physical card number.

Can I get a virtual card number before I arrive in the United States? Yes, with Zolve. You can complete the application, get approved, and receive a virtual card number while still abroad. The card is usable for online purchases immediately, making it one of the only options available before you land in the U.S.
