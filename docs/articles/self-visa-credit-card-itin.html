Self Visa With an ITIN? Self Requires an SSN (2026)

By Editorial Staff · Secured & Starter Cards · Updated November 16, 2026

Advertiser disclosure:ITIN Credit Card is an independent resource. Some links on this page are from partners who compensate us, which may affect which products we feature and where. This does not change our editorial guidance and is never a cost to you. See our full disclosure.

Quick Answer

No. Self's help center says the Self Visa Credit Card requires permanent U.S. residency or citizenship with a Social Security Number, and joining Self at all requires an SSN. An ITIN alone does not qualify. If you only have an ITIN, look at issuers whose own applications take an ITIN, such as Citi's online form.

Correction (November 16, 2026): the first version of this article, published November 11, said the Self Visa Credit Card accepts an ITIN in place of an SSN. That was wrong. Self’s own eligibility page requires “a permanent US residency or citizenship with a Social Security Number.” The original draft repeated a claim that our own issuer checks had already ruled out in August, and we have rewritten the article around what Self’s pages actually say.

If you searched for the Self Visa because you only have an ITIN, the short answer is that Self is not built for you yet. Below is what Self requires, who it does work for, and where an ITIN holder can apply instead.

”Does Self accept an ITIN for the Self Visa?”

A question we hear often: no, not according to Self.

Self’s help center article on eligibility for the Self Visa lists three conditions: share your income and expenses, have permanent U.S. residency or citizenship with a Social Security Number, and be able to fund a minimum $100 security deposit. An ITIN does not appear anywhere in that list, and no alternative to the SSN is offered.

The same rule applies one step earlier. Self’s identity verification page says that to join Self at all, you must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident, or have an H1, L1 or student visa and a Social Security Number, and be at least 18. That covers the Credit Builder Account, which is the product many people assume is the ITIN-friendly entry point. It is not.

Self also notes that meeting the minimum requirements does not guarantee an offer. So even applicants with an SSN can be declined.

”Why do so many sites say Self works with an ITIN?”

Part of the confusion comes from Self itself. Its blog publishes general guides about ITINs, credit reports and building credit as an immigrant, and those posts sometimes mention that other banks and card issuers accept an ITIN. That is advice about the market, not a statement about Self’s own products.

The other part is copying. Once one comparison article lists Self as “ITIN OK,” others repeat it without opening Self’s application. Our first draft of this page did the same thing, which is exactly why it was wrong. When an issuer’s own help center and a third-party list disagree, trust the issuer.

The deposit and income pieces are easy for most people. The SSN line is the one that stops ITIN holders, and nothing on Self’s pages says it can be swapped for an ITIN.

”If I qualify later, what does the Self Visa cost?”

Readers frequently ask: this matters if you expect to receive an SSN, for example after a green card or a work permit.

According to Self’s Self Visa product page, the card has a $100 minimum security deposit, a $0 annual fee in the first year for new customers, a $25 annual fee after that, and a 27.74% variable APR. Self says applying does not require a hard credit inquiry, and that you can fund the deposit with a debit card, a bank account, or savings from an active Self Credit Builder Account. You no longer have to open the Credit Builder Account first.

The usual secured-card rule applies: pay the statement balance in full every month. At a 27.74% APR, any balance you carry costs more than the card is worth as a credit-building tool.

”Where can I apply with only an ITIN instead?”

Look for issuers whose own application asks for an “SSN or ITIN,” not for a list that says so. Our verified issuer list tracks this card by card. A few starting points from issuers’ own pages:

  • Citi: its online application has a field that takes either an SSN or an ITIN. See our Citi with an ITIN guide.
  • Capital One: says ITIN holders may be able to get some of its cards, which is a hedge, not a promise. See our Capital One with an ITIN guide.
  • Credit unions: many apply case by case. Our bank-by-bank application guide covers which ones to call.

If you are starting from zero U.S. history, our starter card rankings walk through the order to apply in, and the secured vs unsecured comparison explains deposits and graduation.

”What if I get an SSN later?”

Then Self becomes a real option, and the card history you built under your ITIN is worth carrying over rather than starting again. Our guide on moving your credit card history from an ITIN to an SSN covers how that works.

Until then, apply where your ITIN is accepted on the application itself, and confirm Self’s rules with Self directly (its support line is 1-877-883-0999) before you spend time on its sign-up flow.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get the Self Visa Credit Card with only an ITIN and no SSN? No. Self’s eligibility page for the Self Visa requires permanent U.S. residency or citizenship with a Social Security Number. An ITIN is not listed as an alternative, so an ITIN-only applicant does not meet Self’s stated requirements.

Can I open a Self Credit Builder Account with an ITIN? Not according to Self. Its help center says that to join Self you must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident, or hold an H1, L1 or student visa, and have a Social Security Number. That rule covers the Credit Builder Account too.

I have a work visa and an SSN. Can I use Self? Possibly. Self’s identity page lists H1, L1 and student visa holders with a Social Security Number as eligible to join. You would apply with your SSN, not your ITIN, and approval is still subject to Self’s other checks.

What does the Self Visa cost if I do qualify? Self’s product page lists a $100 minimum security deposit, a $0 annual fee for the first year for new customers, a $25 annual fee after that, and a 27.74% variable APR. Confirm current terms on Self’s site before applying.

Does applying for the Self Visa require a hard credit inquiry? Self says applying for the Self Visa does not require a hard credit inquiry. That only matters if you meet the SSN requirement first.

What should I apply for instead if I only have an ITIN? Start with issuers whose own applications accept an ITIN. Citi’s online application has an SSN-or-ITIN field, and Capital One says ITIN holders may be able to get some of its cards. Our verified issuer list covers the rest, and Self’s support line (1-877-883-0999) can confirm its own rules.
