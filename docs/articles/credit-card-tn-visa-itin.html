Credit Cards for TN Visa Holders With an ITIN (2026)

Quick Answer

TN visa holders can apply for a credit card using an ITIN at issuers like Capital One, Discover, and OpenSky. TD visa dependents, who cannot get an SSN, rely exclusively on an ITIN. Secured cards are the most accessible starting point, and several issuers approve applicants with no U.S. credit history.

TN visa holders from Canada and Mexico are often surprised to find that the U.S. credit system treats them like complete strangers, even if they arrive with a strong income and a solid financial record at home. The good news: an ITIN opens real doors. Several major issuers accept an ITIN on a credit card application, and a handful of secured cards approve applicants with zero U.S. credit history. Here is exactly what works, what to watch for, and how to start building credit from day one on a TN or TD status.

Wait, who exactly gets a TN visa, and why does it matter for credit cards?

A TN visa (Trade NAFTA, now governed by the USMCA) is a nonimmigrant work status available to Canadian and Mexican professionals in designated occupations such as engineers, accountants, scientists, and lawyers. Only noncitizens authorized to work in the United States by the Department of Homeland Security can receive a Social Security number, and that includes Canadian and Mexican citizens holding TN status under the USMCA.

That detail matters enormously for credit. A TN primary holder can apply for an SSN once they have an Employment Authorization Document or approved status, but they often arrive weeks or months before that SSN is issued. During that gap, and sometimes indefinitely if they prefer to use an ITIN, they need a card that accepts something other than an SSN.

TD visa dependents (typically the spouse or minor children of a TN holder) face a harder situation. Dependent TD visa holders without employment authorization cannot obtain an SSN but may qualify for an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number from the IRS for tax purposes. That means an ITIN is the only tax identification number a TD spouse will ever hold, making ITIN-friendly issuers the only path to a credit card in their own name.

What documents do I need to apply with my ITIN on a TN visa?

This one comes up a lot: the document list is slightly longer for TN and TD holders than for permanent residents, but nothing on it is hard to gather.

You will generally need your ITIN (a nine-digit number starting with 9, issued by the IRS on Form W-7), a valid passport, your TN visa stamp or I-94 printout confirming current status, a U.S. residential address, and proof of income. For TN holders, an employment offer letter or recent pay stub works well as income proof. TD dependents with no U.S. income should be prepared to document a spouse’s income or start with a secured card that does not require employment.

If you are applying before your ITIN is processed, note that the IRS typically takes 6 to 11 weeks to process Form W-7. Plan ahead so your ITIN arrives before you start applying for cards.

Will my TN visa expiration date hurt my approval chances?

Readers frequently ask: issuers do look at visa validity, and some traditional banks flag applications where the visa expires within 12 months. What matters more to most lenders is income and existing U.S. credit history. Some lenders are cautious about near-term visa expirations, but immigrant-friendly fintechs tend to focus on income potential rather than visa type.

TN status is technically issued in one-year or three-year increments and can be renewed indefinitely, but the paperwork says “one year” and that can trigger automated red flags at conservative underwriters. Secured cards sidestep this problem almost entirely because the deposit reduces the issuer’s risk, and programs like OpenSky or Capital One’s secured line do not make visa duration a primary factor.

If you want an unsecured card, applying in-branch at a bank where you already hold a checking account tends to produce better results than an online-only application. A banker who can see your income deposits and speak with a human underwriter can override an automated denial tied to visa expiration.

Can I get a credit card immediately after arriving, before I have any U.S. credit history?

Yes, but you need to target the right products. Credit scoring in the U.S. is local. Your excellent history in a home country usually does not carry over to American lenders. When you apply for a card, the bank pulls your U.S. credit file, and if that file is blank, the system often spits out a denial even if you earn a great salary.

The workaround for new TN arrivals is to start with a no-credit-check secured card. The OpenSky Secured Credit Card is one of the few secured cards that openly approves applicants without an SSN, with an ITIN accepted and no credit check during the application. OpenSky reports your account activity to all three major credit bureaus every month. A secured card with consistent on-time payments creates a U.S. credit file from scratch.

With consistent use of credit-builder tools and on-time payments, many newcomers reach a usable FICO score in 3 to 6 months. A strong score of 700 or higher typically takes 12-24 months. Running two or three accounts simultaneously, for example a secured card plus being an authorized user on a partner’s card, can accelerate that timeline.

Alternatively, Zolve is worth considering if you want an unsecured option. It is an unsecured U.S. credit card with no annual fee that does not require an SSN and accepts a passport or ITIN, and you can apply and receive a virtual card before you even arrive in the U.S.

What happens to my ITIN credit history when I eventually get an SSN?

Many TN holders are promoted, change status to H-1B or green card, and eventually receive an SSN. Do not assume your credit history transfers automatically.

Contact each credit card issuer to update your records when you receive an SSN, as this also helps your credit history consolidate under your new SSN. After updating with each issuer, go a step further: contact Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion to link your ITIN credit file to your new SSN, which preserves the credit history you built as an ITIN holder.

Skipping this step is a costly mistake. Once your ITIN has been rescinded you need to request that your accounts and entire credit history be transferred to the SSN. Attempting to use both simultaneously is likely to cause issues, and accounts or identities may be flagged for possible fraud.

How do I compare my actual card options as a TN or TD holder?

A question we hear often: the comparison below focuses on the two most common starting points.

Secured card path (recommended for most new arrivals)

A secured card requires a refundable deposit, usually between $200 and $500, that becomes your credit limit. Because the deposit covers the issuer’s risk, approval rates are higher and income requirements are more flexible. After 12-24 months of on-time payments, most secured cards offer graduation to an unsecured product and return your deposit. See our full breakdown of how upgrading from a secured to an unsecured card works with an ITIN.

Unsecured card path (for applicants with income documentation or pre-existing banking relationships)

Banks and credit card issuers can legally accept an ITIN under the Patriot Act’s Know Your Customer rules. Not every issuer chooses to do so, but a growing number do. If you arrive with a strong income, an established relationship at a bank that accepts ITINs, or you apply through a fintech like Zolve that evaluates alternative data, an unsecured card is possible from the start. Expect a lower initial credit limit until you establish a U.S. credit file.

Frequently asked questions

Can a TN visa holder get a credit card without an SSN? Yes. While TN visa primary holders who are authorized to work can obtain an SSN, many arrive in the U.S. before their SSN is processed. During that window, and in some cases permanently, an ITIN works as a substitute at issuers such as Capital One, Discover, and OpenSky.

Can a TD visa dependent get a credit card with an ITIN? Yes. TD visa dependents are not authorized to work in the U.S. and therefore cannot obtain an SSN. An ITIN is their only option for a tax identification number, and ITIN-accepting issuers will process their applications on that basis.

What documents do TN visa holders need to apply for a credit card with an ITIN? Typically: your ITIN, valid passport, TN or TD visa stamp or I-94 record, U.S. address, and proof of income. Some issuers may also request a recent pay stub or employment letter. Requirements vary by issuer, so confirm before applying.

Does my TN visa expiration date hurt my credit card application? It can. Some traditional issuers factor visa expiration into their risk assessment. Immigrant-friendly issuers and most secured-card programs focus primarily on income and deposit amount rather than visa duration, making them a safer first application.

Once I get an SSN on a TN visa, do I need to update my credit card issuer? Yes. Contact each card issuer to update your tax ID from ITIN to SSN. You should also notify all three credit bureaus so your ITIN-based credit history links to your new SSN, preserving the credit you already built.
