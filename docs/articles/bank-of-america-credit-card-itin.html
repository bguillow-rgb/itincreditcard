Bank of America Credit Card With an ITIN (2026)

By Research Desk · Issuers & Approval · Published November 18, 2026

Quick Answer

Yes, Bank of America accepts an ITIN for credit card applications, but ITIN holders must apply in person at a branch financial center, not online. The BankAmericard Secured and Customized Cash Rewards Secured are the most accessible starting points, requiring a $200 minimum deposit and no annual fee.

Bank of America is one of the largest credit card issuers in the United States, and it explicitly accepts an ITIN in place of a Social Security number on credit card applications. The catch: the process looks different than it does for SSN holders. You can apply online or at a branch with a Social Security number, but with an ITIN you have to go in person. That in-branch requirement trips up a lot of applicants, so this guide walks through exactly what to expect before you walk through the door.

Does Bank of America actually accept an ITIN for a credit card, or is that a rumor?

A question we hear often: many readers have seen conflicting posts online, with some saying Bank of America turned them away. Here is what the evidence shows.

Bank of America accepts an ITIN in place of an SSN, and also accepts a passport number as an alternative form of identification. Multiple major credit resources confirm this, and community data backs it up. In-branch applications at Bank of America with an ITIN have led to approvals.

That said, the bank’s own card pages say very little about ITIN eligibility directly. Bank of America publishes its card terms in detail but publishes nothing about ITIN on those pages, so the absence of a statement is not a denial. The requirement is simply that you apply in person. Bank of America credit card approval requirements include a Social Security number or ITIN, and applying with the latter requires visiting a Bank of America financial center.

Why can’t I just apply online like everyone else?

This one comes up a lot. The frustration is understandable, especially if you are comfortable applying for everything digitally.

Bank of America’s online application flow is built around an SSN input field. When no SSN is entered, the system does not route the application forward in the standard way. ITIN-only applicants need to visit a branch, which makes Bank of America the least remote-friendly option among major issuers.

This is not necessarily a disadvantage in practice. Branch bankers have more flexibility to review your full financial picture, ask follow-up questions, and document your identity properly. For Bank of America specifically, branch applications have significantly higher ITIN approval rates than online attempts. Going in person is not just the only route available to you: it is also the route most likely to result in an approval. If you are comparing this to other issuers, our guide to applying online vs. in-branch with an ITIN covers the trade-offs in more detail.

Readers frequently ask: whether only the secured card is available, or whether unsecured rewards cards are also on the table.

Here is a comparison of Bank of America cards commonly pursued by ITIN holders:

For ITIN holders starting from zero U.S. credit history, the two secured cards are the practical entry point. A refundable deposit of $200 to $5,000 sets your credit line, there is no annual fee, and monthly reporting builds your track record toward a possible deposit return or upgrade after about 12 months.

The Customized Cash Rewards Secured adds a rewards layer on top of the credit-building function. It earns cash back on purchases, requires a security deposit of $200 to $5,000 (used in combination with your income and ability to pay to establish your credit line), and charges no annual fee. That is a genuinely rare combination for a secured card.

For the unsecured cards, approval is realistic once you have built some U.S. credit history. On the unsecured side, the record includes an approval at a $1,000 limit after fifteen months, and a denial that was reversed to a $10,000 card after a reconsideration call. Worth keeping in mind if your first decision is a no: calling the credit card reconsideration line often changes outcomes at Bank of America.

How much deposit do I need, and do I get it back?

Bank of America’s BankAmericard Secured application accepts a Social Security number or ITIN, with a $200 minimum deposit up to $5,000 and no annual fee.

Your deposit is not locked away permanently. The bank conducts periodic reviews of your credit history, and if you have demonstrated good credit habits by Bank of America’s standards, the issuer will return your security deposit and let you keep using the card. That said, Bank of America is explicit in its terms: not all customers will qualify for a deposit return.

Putting down more than the minimum $200 is worth considering. Bank of America says the maximum credit limit is determined by the deposit, your income, and your ability to pay. A higher deposit combined with a solid stated income tends to produce a higher starting credit line, which helps keep your credit utilization low from day one.

What do I actually bring to the branch appointment?

A question we hear often: readers want a concrete checklist so they are not turned away at the door.

Documentation includes a foreign passport, your ITIN letter, and proof of a U.S. address. The branch visit goes more smoothly when you have all three ready before you sit down with a banker.

Here is a fuller checklist:

  • ITIN letter: Your IRS CP565 notice or W-7 approval letter, the original document, not just the number written on a piece of paper.
  • Foreign passport: This is your primary government-issued photo ID.
  • Proof of U.S. address: A recent utility bill, bank statement, or lease agreement showing your name and current address. If your passport address differs from your U.S. address, this secondary document is especially important.
  • Deposit funds: Know how much you plan to put down (at least $200) and have it accessible from a U.S. bank account.
  • Income information: You will be asked for your total gross annual income. Have a figure ready. Other requirements include personal and financial information such as your name, address, birthdate, annual income, and sources for that income.

You must also be 18 years of age or older to be approved. If under 21, an independent income source is required.

Does an existing Bank of America bank account really help?

Yes, and this is one of the most actionable steps you can take before applying. Bank of America accepts an ITIN for credit card applications, and if you already have a checking or savings account with them, your approval odds improve significantly.

The logic is straightforward. The bank can see your deposit history, average balances, and how responsibly you manage money, giving underwriters a data point beyond your thin or non-existent U.S. credit file. Opening a Bank of America checking account first, even a basic one, meaningfully changes your odds.

Starting in May 2026, simply having a Bank of America checking account enrolls you in the new Bank of America Rewards program, which means everyone qualifies for at least a 10% credit card rewards bonus on top of whatever cash back they earn, with no minimum balance required. That is a real benefit that extends beyond approval odds into everyday card value.

What happens after I get the card? How do I build toward an unsecured product?

Once your secured Bank of America card is open and reporting, the path forward is consistent. Pay your statement balance in full each month (or at minimum, never miss a payment), keep your utilization below 30%, and let the account age. The secured card is a solid starting point for ITIN holders with no U.S. credit history.

After 12 months or so of responsible use, Bank of America may proactively return your deposit and convert your account, or you can request a review. If you eventually obtain a Social Security number, our guide on what happens to your credit cards when you switch from an ITIN to an SSN explains how to protect the credit history you built.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for a Bank of America credit card online with an ITIN? No. Bank of America’s online application only accepts a Social Security number. ITIN holders must visit a local branch financial center in person to complete their application.

Which Bank of America cards accept an ITIN? The BankAmericard Secured and the Customized Cash Rewards Secured are the most reliably documented options for ITIN holders. Both require a $200 minimum deposit and charge no annual fee.

Does having a Bank of America bank account improve my approval odds? Yes. Having an existing Bank of America checking or savings account significantly improves your approval odds for their credit cards. Opening one before applying is a commonly recommended step.

What documents do I need to apply at a Bank of America branch with an ITIN? Bring your ITIN letter (IRS CP565 or W-7 approval notice), a valid foreign passport as your primary ID, and proof of your U.S. address such as a utility bill or lease agreement.

Can I eventually get an unsecured Bank of America card with an ITIN? Yes. Bank of America periodically reviews secured card accounts. After roughly 12 months of responsible use and on-time payments, the bank may return your deposit and transition you to an unsecured product.
