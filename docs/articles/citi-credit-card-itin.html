Citi Credit Card With an ITIN (2026 Guide)

By Research Desk · Issuers & Approval · Published October 15, 2026

Advertiser disclosure:ITIN Credit Card is an independent resource. Some links on this page are from partners who compensate us, which may affect which products we feature and where. This does not change our editorial guidance and is never a cost to you. See our full disclosure.

Quick Answer

Yes, Citi accepts an ITIN in place of an SSN on credit card applications. The Citi Secured Mastercard is the most accessible option, with a $200-$2,500 deposit, no annual fee, and all-three-bureau reporting. Having an existing Citi checking or savings account significantly improves approval odds for ITIN holders.

Citi is one of the five largest U.S. card issuers, which makes it a natural target for ITIN holders who want a recognizable name on their card. The good news is concrete: Citi’s live application identity field is labeled “Social Security Number or ITIN,” so this isn’t a rumor or recycled blog copy. The real question isn’t whether Citi accepts ITINs but which cards are realistic for your profile and how to get through the application without hitting a wall.

Does Citi actually let you apply with an ITIN?

A question we hear often: readers see Citi listed on “ITIN-friendly” roundups but want to know if that acceptance is genuine or just blog copy repeated from 2022.

Citi’s Secured Mastercard is a no-annual-fee secured card that reports to the major bureaus, and Citi’s own requirements list an SSN or ITIN among the identity fields. That’s first-party confirmation, not hearsay.

For unsecured products, the picture is more conditional. Citi accepts ITINs for those applications too, but the process goes more smoothly if you already have a Citi checking or savings account. Compared to Capital One or Chase, Citi’s ITIN acceptance has a bit more friction baked in. A banking relationship isn’t technically required, but in practice it’s close to one.

Start with the secured card or open a Citi checking account first. Either move puts you on solid ground before you apply.

What does the Citi Secured Mastercard actually cost?

This one comes up a lot: people want to know the real all-in cost before they commit a deposit.

You choose a deposit between $200 and $2,500 in $100 increments, there’s no annual fee, and Citi reports to all three bureaus. That’s a genuinely competitive fee structure. For context, OpenSky charges $35 per year and skips the credit check entirely, while Citi runs a credit check but charges nothing annually. Different tradeoffs, worth knowing before you decide.

The APR on the Citi Secured Mastercard runs approximately 27.74% variable as of mid-2026, per Citi’s product disclosures. That rate only matters if you carry a balance. Pay in full each month and you owe nothing in interest, which is also the fastest way to build credit. For a full breakdown of keeping your card costs near zero, see how to use an ITIN credit card responsibly.

One thing Citi doesn’t publish: a firm timeline for upgrading from secured to unsecured. Capital One and Discover both have disclosed review windows (roughly six and seven to eight months, respectively). Citi’s upgrade path is more discretionary, so go in treating the secured card as a 12-24 month commitment unless you get a proactive offer.

Do I have to go to a branch to apply with my ITIN?

Readers frequently ask: whether the online application actually works or whether ITIN holders always get redirected.

Applying with an ITIN online is generally the same experience as applying with an SSN. You may need to supply additional documents, proof of income and a U.S. address being the most common, but the form itself accepts ITINs without issue.

That said, Citi does route some ITIN-only applicants to phone or branch verification. The practical fix is to have a Citi checking or savings account open before you apply. When the system already has your ITIN on file from the banking side, the credit card application moves through online without extra friction. If you don’t have a Citi account yet, applying in-branch or by phone gives you a live representative who can manually process your ITIN and supporting documents without the application timing out or throwing an error.

Documents to have ready: your ITIN assignment letter or most recent tax return showing your ITIN, a valid government-issued photo ID (passport or consular ID), proof of a U.S. address such as a lease or utility bill, and recent proof of income such as pay stubs or a bank statement.

How do my approval odds compare at Citi versus other issuers?

Citi is not the most lenient major bank for ITIN holders with zero U.S. credit history. That’s worth saying plainly. For most ITIN holders who can fund a $300 deposit, OpenSky’s Plus card is a strong alternative: no annual fee, no monthly fee, no credit check, all-three-bureau reporting, and an 89% approval rate.

Citi runs a credit check, so if your U.S. credit file is thin or nonexistent, a denial is possible even with a valid ITIN and solid income. Your best odds come from applying at a bank where you already have a checking or savings account. That logic applies directly to Citi: a few months of relationship history makes a real difference.

If Citi denies your application, don’t panic. Our guide on what to do when a credit card is denied with an ITIN covers reconsideration calls, alternative issuers, and how to strengthen your application before you try again.

What happens to my Citi card if I eventually get an SSN?

A question we hear often: ITIN holders on a path to permanent residency want to know whether their Citi card history carries over.

The short answer is yes. When you receive an SSN, you notify Citi and update your account. The card stays open and your full payment history stays intact on your credit reports. A 2026 Experian white paper found that ITIN holders carry a lower debt-to-income ratio and fewer accounts with payments past due compared to SSN holders, which means the credit behavior you build with your Citi card now contributes to a strong profile well before you ever hold an SSN. For a detailed walkthrough of the transition process, see what happens to your credit cards when you get an SSN after ITIN.

Does Citi report to all three credit bureaus?

Yes. Citi reports account activity to Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion every month. Each on-time payment and every low-utilization billing cycle builds your file at all three agencies at once. For most credit decisions, including apartment applications and eventual unsecured card upgrades, lenders pull at least one of those three files. Open a secured card, pay on time every month, and keep utilization below 30%. After 12 months of on-time payments, scores typically land in the 680-720 range, which opens the door to rewards cards from Capital One, Chase, or American Express. The same trajectory applies with Citi as the launching pad.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Citi accept an ITIN instead of an SSN for credit cards? Yes. Citi’s live application form labels the identity field “Social Security Number or ITIN,” and the Citi Secured Mastercard product page explicitly lists ITIN as an accepted identifier. Some unsecured cards also accept ITINs, especially for applicants with an existing Citi banking relationship.

Which Citi credit card is easiest to get with an ITIN? The Citi Secured Mastercard is the easiest entry point. It requires no prior U.S. credit history, has no annual fee, and accepts a deposit of $200 to $2,500. Approval requires a credit check, but the secured nature of the card means the bar is lower than for unsecured products.

Do I need to visit a Citi branch to apply with an ITIN? Not always, but it helps. Citi’s online application accepts ITINs directly. However, some ITIN-only applicants are routed to phone or branch verification. Having a Citi checking account first often lets you complete the whole process online without extra steps.

What deposit does the Citi Secured Mastercard require? The minimum deposit is $200. You can increase it in $100 increments up to $2,500. Your credit line equals your deposit amount. Citi reviews accounts for graduation to an unsecured card, but it does not follow a fixed public timeline the way Capital One does.

Does the Citi Secured Mastercard report to all three credit bureaus? Yes. Citi reports to Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion monthly. That means on-time payments and responsible utilization with this card build credit history across all three bureaus from your very first billing cycle.
