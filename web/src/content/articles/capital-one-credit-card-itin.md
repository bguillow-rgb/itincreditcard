Capital One is one of the more consistently ITIN-friendly major card issuers in the country. You can get a Capital One credit card without an SSN. Capital One will accept an ITIN in place of a Social Security number, and that covers every product in the lineup, from secured starter cards to travel rewards cards. The real question is not whether Capital One accepts your ITIN, but which card fits where you actually are in your credit history right now.

## Does Capital One actually accept an ITIN, or is this just a rumor I keep reading?

*A question we hear often:* Yes, and it's confirmed directly by Capital One. All Capital One credit cards accept an ITIN in place of an SSN. No separate ITIN application, no special branch visit. You enter your ITIN in the Tax ID field when you apply online, over the phone, or in person.

One distinction worth making: acceptance is not the same as approval. ITIN acceptance means the issuer takes the application. Each issuer's normal eligibility criteria (income, history, deposit) still apply, and policies can change without notice. Capital One will read your application, but you still need to meet its income and creditworthiness requirements for whichever card you choose.

## Which Capital One cards can I realistically get with an ITIN?

*This one comes up a lot:* The answer depends on how much U.S. credit history you already have. The table below maps the main Capital One cards to realistic ITIN applicant profiles.

| Card | Type | Approx. Credit Needed | Deposit | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum Secured Mastercard | Secured | None required | $49-$200 refundable | Zero U.S. history |
| QuicksilverOne Cash Rewards | Unsecured | Fair (580+) | None | Some U.S. history |
| Capital One Quicksilver Secured | Secured | None required | $200 refundable | Rewards while building |
| Capital One Venture Rewards | Unsecured | Good-Excellent (720+) | None | 12-24 months of history |
| Chase Freedom Rise (comparison) | Unsecured | Limited credit | None | See our [Chase ITIN guide](/articles/chase-credit-card-itin) |

Capital One accepts ITIN applications for Quicksilver, Platinum, and the Secured Mastercard. For someone arriving with no U.S. credit file, the Platinum Secured or Quicksilver Secured is where nearly everyone should start.

## How does the pre-approval tool work with an ITIN, and will it hurt my credit score?

Capital One's pre-approval tool at capitalone.com accepts an ITIN. The check does not affect your credit score and gives you a read on your approval odds before you submit a full application. It typically takes about 90 seconds. Capital One's eligibility page asks for information like income and date of birth, and it includes an ITIN field.

Use the pre-approval step before anything else. It shows which cards Capital One is likely to offer you without leaving a hard inquiry on your credit report. If Capital One surfaces the Platinum Secured or Quicksilver Secured during pre-approval, apply for that card. If nothing comes back, it usually points to an income or debt concern, not your ITIN itself.

## What do I need to actually apply?

*Readers frequently ask:* Gather these items before you start the application so you don't get stuck mid-form:

- Your ITIN from the IRS letter (the nine-digit number beginning with 9)
- Legal name exactly as it appears on your IRS correspondence
- U.S. mailing address (no P.O. boxes)
- Date of birth
- Annual income, including employment income, self-employment, or any other household income you can legally report
- A U.S. bank account to fund the deposit if you apply for a secured card

You need an ITIN, a foreign passport, a U.S. mailing address, and a U.S. bank account to fund any deposit. ## What deposit do I need for the Capital One Platinum Secured Card?

The deposit requirement is one of the most-searched details about this card. Capital One determines your minimum deposit based on a brief review of your application. It can start as low as $49. Many applicants without any U.S. credit history receive a $200 minimum deposit requirement, which is standard across secured cards. The deposit is fully refundable when you close the account in good standing or graduate to an unsecured card.

There's a strict deadline attached. If Capital One approves a secured card, you must make the minimum deposit within 35 days of approval, per Capital One's April 2026 guidance. Mark that date on your calendar the moment you receive your approval email. A missed deadline means your application lapses entirely.

Your initial credit limit equals the amount you deposit, so putting in more than the minimum gives you a higher limit from day one. Many ITIN holders who want a faster path to a usable limit deposit $500 or even $1,000 upfront. ## What about unsecured Capital One cards for ITIN holders?

*A question we hear often:* Unsecured Capital One cards are accessible to ITIN holders, but they require some U.S. credit history first.

The Capital One QuicksilverOne Cash Rewards Credit Card is a solid cash-back option for people with average credit or who are actively building credit history. You can apply online using your ITIN instead of an SSN. QuicksilverOne is typically the next card after you've spent 12-18 months on a secured card and built a FICO score in the fair range (580 or above).

At the top end, the Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card also allows ITIN applications and offers a $300 credit for hotel and vacation rental bookings through Capital One Travel in the first cardholder year. Venture targets applicants with good to excellent credit, though. It typically requires one or more years of U.S. credit history and a FICO score of 720 or higher. Most ITIN holders can't get it as a first card. The path is: start with secured or Platinum, build history, then apply for Venture after 12-18 months.

## Will Capital One report my card activity to all three credit bureaus?

Yes. Capital One reports to Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion each month, whether you applied with an ITIN or an SSN. Having an ITIN does not lock you out of the U.S. credit system. Several major banks and credit unions accept ITIN applications, and building credit follows the same principles regardless of which number you use. Every on-time payment you make with a Capital One card builds your file at all three bureaus at once, which is exactly what you need to qualify for better cards over time. For more on how bureau reporting works for ITIN cardholders specifically, see our guide on [ITIN credit card bureau reporting](/articles/itin-credit-card-credit-bureau-reporting).

## What if Capital One denies me?

Capital One will send a written denial notice listing the specific reasons. Common reasons for ITIN holders include insufficient income, too many recent credit applications, or no U.S. credit history on file.

Read the denial letter carefully first. An ITIN is not an approval. The number lets participating issuers identify you, but approval still depends on income and debt. Capital One says even its secured card has income-and-debt conditions despite not requiring a credit score.

If income was the issue, check whether you reported all eligible income sources. If history was the issue, the [OpenSky Secured Card](/articles/secured-credit-card-with-itin) has no credit check at all and can help you start building a U.S. file. After six to twelve months of consistent, on-time payments on any reporting card, your odds with Capital One improve substantially. The Capital One Platinum Secured Card is the most accessible starting point. It requires a refundable deposit (as low as $49 in some cases, typically $200) and does not require an established U.S. credit score, making it well-suited for ITIN holders building credit from zero.

Can I use Capital One's pre-approval tool with an ITIN?
Yes. Capital One's pre-approval page accepts an ITIN in the tax ID field. The check uses a soft inquiry and will not affect your credit score. It typically takes about 90 seconds and shows which cards you are likely to qualify for.

How long after Capital One approves me do I have to fund the secured card deposit?
Capital One requires you to fund the deposit within 35 days of approval. Missing that window means your application lapses and you would need to reapply. Set a calendar reminder as soon as you receive your approval notice.

Can ITIN holders eventually get the Capital One Venture card?
The Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card targets applicants with established U.S. credit, generally 12-24 months of history and a FICO score around 720 or higher. Most ITIN holders should start with a secured or entry-level card and work toward Venture after building a solid file.
