Discover has long been one of the more ITIN-friendly major card issuers in the U.S., accepting your Individual Taxpayer Identification Number in place of a Social Security Number on its application. But 2026 brought a real change: Capital One completed its acquisition of Discover in 2025, and Discover stopped accepting Discover it Secured applications on June 2, 2026. If you have been searching for a Discover card with an ITIN, here is exactly what still works and what your best next moves are.

---

## Does Discover actually accept an ITIN on its application?

Yes, and this is one of the things Discover has been clearer about than most major issuers. You will need your SSN or ITIN ready before you start the application. Just enter your ITIN in the field labeled "Social Security Number or ITIN" and the rest of the application works the same as it would for anyone else.

This matters because many ITIN holders assume "SSN field" means SSN only, and they abandon the application before finishing. Don't do that. The field is shared on purpose.

---

## What happened to the Discover it Secured card, and does it affect my account?

*A question we hear often:*

Capital One acquired Discover in 2025, and applications for the secured card closed in June 2026, with plans to relaunch later. If you already have a Discover it Secured card, your account is unaffected. You keep your card, your rewards, and your account history. The pause only stops new applications.

The secured card was widely regarded as one of the better entry-level options for ITIN holders. It earned real cash-back rewards, charged no annual fee, and offered a credible path to an unsecured line of credit. Its key specs before the pause: a $200-$2,500 refundable deposit range, 2% cash back at gas stations and restaurants (on up to $1,000 combined per quarter), 1% on everything else, and no foreign transaction fee. If the card relaunches, those terms are the benchmark to compare against.

For now, ITIN holders who need a secured card must look elsewhere. See the alternatives section below and our guide to [secured credit cards for ITIN holders](/secured-credit-cards) for vetted options that are accepting applications today.

## Which Discover cards can I still apply for with an ITIN right now?

*This one comes up a lot:*

The unsecured Discover lineup remains open. Discover accepts ITINs as an alternative to SSNs across both its secured and unsecured cards, though the secured card is currently paused. Unsecured options include:

| Discover Card | ITIN Accepted | Approx. Score Needed | Annual Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discover it Cash Back | Yes | ~670+ FICO | $0 | 5% rotating categories, 1% all else |
| Discover it Chrome | Yes | ~670+ FICO | $0 | 2% gas & restaurants, 1% all else |
| Discover it Student Cash Back | Yes | No minimum required | $0 | For enrolled students |
| Discover it Secured | Yes (PAUSED) | No minimum | $0 | New applications paused since June 2, 2026 |

Most standard Discover cards generally require a credit score of 700 or higher, while student cards are designed for applicants with limited or no credit history. If you have been in the U.S. for at least 6-12 months and have been building credit with a starter card, the Discover it Cash Back or Chrome are realistic targets. Brand new with no U.S. credit file? A student card (if you are enrolled) or an alternative issuer is the smarter first step.

One more rule worth knowing: Discover automatically denies applications from people who already have two open Discover cards or who opened a new Discover card within the previous 12 months.

## What documents do I need when applying for a Discover card with an ITIN?

*Readers frequently ask:*

Gather these before you start. The online application moves quickly and you will not want to pause mid-way:

- **Your ITIN:** The nine-digit number from the IRS. Enter it in the SSN/ITIN field.
- **U.S. address:** A current, verifiable mailing address in the United States.
- **Income information:** Annual income from any source, including self-employment, stipends, or household income. Issuers are required under the CARD Act to verify that you can repay.
- **Government-issued photo ID:** A passport or consular ID (matrícula) is standard for ITIN holders without a U.S. driver's license.

Applicants should prepare identification, income, address, employment, and housing information before starting. The application typically takes about 10 minutes to complete.

After submitting, outcomes include instant approval, pending review, or denial. Discover must notify applicants of an approval or denial decision within 30 days of receiving a completed application.

## What are my approval odds as an ITIN holder with no U.S. credit history?

Being direct: approval for an unsecured Discover card with zero U.S. credit history is difficult. Direct approval is possible but uncommon, with roughly a 20-30% approval rate. Most ITIN holders are better served by starting with the secured card and graduating from there. Since the secured card is currently paused, the practical strategy in 2026 is to build 6-12 months of history with an ITIN-accepting issuer first, then apply to Discover.

If you are an international student enrolled at a U.S. school, the Discover it Student cards carry lower approval thresholds and are a more realistic entry point. Under the Credit CARD Act of 2009, applicants under 21 must show independent income or have a co-signer, so students in that age range should be ready to document a stipend, part-time job, or work-study award. According to the CFPB, qualifying income can include wages, salaries, tips, and regular allowances from another person.

## How do I use a pre-qualify tool so I do not waste a hard inquiry?

Discover's pre-approval tool uses a soft credit inquiry that does not affect your credit score. A full application triggers a hard inquiry. Use Discover's pre-qualification page before submitting a formal application, especially if you are unsure whether your credit profile is strong enough for an unsecured card.

If you are denied after pre-qualifying, or denied on a formal application, you have the right to call Discover's reconsideration line and ask a human underwriter to review your file. ## FAQs

**Does Discover accept an ITIN instead of an SSN?**
Yes. Discover's application has a field for either an SSN or ITIN. You enter your ITIN in that field and proceed normally. This applies to all current Discover consumer card applications.

**Is the Discover it Secured card still available to ITIN holders in 2026?**
No, not currently. Discover paused new Discover it Secured applications on June 2, 2026, following Capital One's acquisition. Existing accounts remain open and unaffected. Discover has indicated the secured card may relaunch, but no date is confirmed.

**What credit score do I need for a Discover card with an ITIN?**
For unsecured Discover cards, aim for a FICO score of approximately 670 or higher with at least 6 months of U.S. credit history. Student cards have lower thresholds. The secured card had no minimum score requirement, but it is currently paused for new applicants.

**What documents does Discover need from an ITIN holder?**
Discover needs your ITIN (entered in the SSN/ITIN field), a U.S. address, and verifiable income. You may also be asked to provide a government-issued photo ID such as a passport or consular ID during identity verification.

**What are the best alternatives to Discover for ITIN holders with no U.S. credit history right now?**
Top alternatives include the Capital One Platinum Secured (accepts ITIN, refundable deposit from $200), the OpenSky Secured Visa (no credit check, ITIN accepted), and the Current Build Card (no hard pull, no SSN required at sign-up). All three report to all three major credit bureaus.
