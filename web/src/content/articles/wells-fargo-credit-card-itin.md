You searched for this because you want a straight answer, so here it is: Wells Fargo is one of the least reliable big banks to try with an ITIN, and the reason is not that they said no. It is that for their own consumer cards, they have not clearly said anything.

We re-checked Wells Fargo's published card applications in August 2026. The only place an ITIN shows up as a documented option is on **Retail Services** applications, the store-financing cards Wells Fargo issues for other brands. On the bank's own cards, Active Cash, Autograph, Reflect, there is no published ITIN policy at all. And the product that older guides keep pointing ITIN applicants toward, the Wells Fargo secured card, **no longer exists for new applicants**.

> **Before you apply anywhere:** a full credit card application triggers a hard inquiry whether you are approved or not. When a bank's ITIN policy is undocumented, call and ask about the specific card first. Two minutes on the phone can save a wasted inquiry on a thin file.

## Can you get a Wells Fargo consumer card with an ITIN?

Nothing in Wells Fargo's published application materials confirms it. That puts their branded cards in a different category than issuers like Chase, American Express, and Capital One, which accept an ITIN in place of an SSN on personal card applications.

Applicant reports on Wells Fargo branded cards are mixed, some people say a branch accepted an ITIN application, others were told an SSN was required. When branch-level execution and published policy do not match, treat the outcome as a coin flip, and do not spend a hard inquiry on a coin flip if you are building a new credit file.

## Where does Wells Fargo actually document ITIN acceptance?

On Retail Services applications. Wells Fargo runs financing programs for furniture chains, jewelers, medical providers, and home-improvement brands, and those application forms include an ITIN option.

A store card is not what most people mean when they search for a Wells Fargo credit card, but it is not worthless either. If the account reports to the credit bureaus, it builds your file the same way a bank card does. The trade-offs are real, though: it works at one retailer, and store financing rates run high. Use it if the purchase was already happening; do not open one just to own a card with Wells Fargo's name on the paperwork.

## What happened to the Wells Fargo secured card?

It was discontinued for new applicants. This matters because the secured card was the standard advice for ITIN holders starting from zero, put down a deposit, get a credit line, build history. Plenty of articles still hand out that advice, and following it leads to a dead application page.

If a secured card is the right move for you, and for most people with no U.S. credit history it is, you want an issuer that both offers one and accepts an ITIN on it. Our [secured card with an ITIN guide](/articles/secured-credit-card-with-itin/) walks through the current options in detail.

## Does an ITIN work for Wells Fargo bank accounts?

Yes, and this is worth knowing even if the credit card is out of reach. Deposit accounts follow different identification rules than credit cards. Wells Fargo branches open checking and savings accounts for customers using an ITIN alongside a passport, consular ID, or other government-issued identification.

That matters for two reasons. First, you may simply need the account. Second, banks are consistently more willing to extend credit to existing customers with account history than to strangers, so if you want a Wells Fargo card eventually, a deposit relationship today is the most credible path to one.

## The honest bottom line

Wells Fargo is not the door to knock on first with an ITIN. Their consumer-card policy is undocumented, their secured card is gone, and the one confirmed ITIN path leads to store financing. If your goal is a first card that builds credit, start with an issuer that says yes in writing, [the starter card rankings](/articles/best-starter-credit-cards-itin-zero-credit-history/) are the shortest route, and let a Wells Fargo card be something you pick up later, once you have a file and a banking relationship that make the answer less of a gamble.
